Ghostly VPN is the official open-source app of the Ghostly VPN service (ghostlynex.fun), built on the Xray core. One tap and your connection is protected.

<b>Highlights</b>
• Connect with a single tap on the ghost — no manual setup.
• Smart server choice: the app measures latency and switches to the fastest server by itself.
• Restricted-network mode: on mobile networks with allow-lists Ghostly moves to servers that keep working, and comes back once the regular connection is stable again.
• Automatic failover when the current server stops responding.
• Protocols: VLESS (Reality, XHTTP, gRPC), VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks and Hysteria2.

<b>Subscriptions</b>
• Add by link, from the clipboard or with a QR code.
• Automatic subscription updates with remaining traffic and expiry shown.
• Compatible with Happ-style subscriptions and plain vless://, vmess://, trojan://, ss://, hysteria2:// links.

<b>For those who want more</b>
• Split tunnelling: choose apps that use the VPN or bypass it.
• Smart routing, custom domain rules, DNS choice, ad blocking, IPv6, MTU, multiplexing, TLS fragmentation, QUIC blocking.
• Local SOCKS5/HTTP proxy with a login and password.
• Start on boot and a Quick Settings tile.

<b>Open source</b>
The source code is published on GitHub under GPL-3.0. No ads, no trackers, no browsing data collected.

Servers in the Netherlands, Germany and Finland come with a Ghostly VPN subscription at ghostlynex.fun or the Telegram bot @GhostlyVPNruBot (first 24 hours free); the Ghostly VPN account is built into the app. Any other VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks or Hysteria2 subscription works too.
